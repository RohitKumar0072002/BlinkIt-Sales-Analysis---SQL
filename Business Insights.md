# Business Insights (SQL Analysis)

## Overview
The analysis reveals patterns in sales distribution, product performance, and outlet efficiency.

---

## Product Insights
- A small number of categories drive the majority of revenue  
- High-performing categories dominate sales distribution  
- Low-performing categories contribute minimally  

---

## Outlet Insights
- Supermarket-type outlets generate higher revenue  
- Tier 3 locations outperform Tier 1 and Tier 2  
- Medium-sized outlets show optimal performance  

---

## Sales Insights
- Sales distribution is uneven across categories  
- Some products show consistent performance while others lag  

---

## Business Impact
- Focus on high-demand categories can significantly improve revenue  
- Optimizing outlet strategy can enhance performance  
- Inventory planning should be demand-driven  

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
- Increase focus on top-performing categories  
- Expand in high-performing locations  
- Reduce inventory for low-performing items
